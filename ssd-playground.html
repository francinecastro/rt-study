The math, step by step

    What to notice

    Move the ISO toward a side, and that side's depth gets smaller. Smaller depth means bigger SSD. The other side does the opposite.

    Try it. Drag the ISO slider all the way left, then all the way right, and watch which tile goes up. Then change the thickness only and see that both SSDs move together.
