The kV tube sits 90° away from the MV head, so the kV image runs along the gold line, not where the gantry points. Want a kV lateral? Put the gantry where the MV would shoot AP or PA. Want a kV AP or PA? Put the gantry at a side. In class, a kV lateral counts for right or left, so gantry 0 or 180 both work on TrueBeam.

The flip trick

HFS
Front on top. Patient right sits at the 270 side.
FFS
Front on top. Right and left swap. Right moves to the 90 side.
HFP
Back on top. Right and left swap. Right moves to the 90 side.
FFP
Back on top. Two swaps cancel, so right is back on the 270 side.
Both machines put 90 on the right of the circle and 270 on the left. Only top and bottom change. TrueBeam top is 0. Trilogy top is 180. An oblique lives in the quarter between its two letters. RAO sits between A and R.
