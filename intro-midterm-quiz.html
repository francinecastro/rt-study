Important Original Quiz Corrections

  • Informed consent: purpose, alternatives, benefits, and risks. Daily dose is not part of that specific consent answer.
  • Abutting-field methods — EXCEPT: penumbra method.
  • Medical accelerator hazards: electrical/mechanical problems, wrong dose, wrong area, wrong beam, and collision are genuine safety hazards.
  • Treatment unit status: found at the treatment console.
  • Parallel opposed gantry pair: 310° and 130° because they differ by 180°.
  • Verify & Record: verifies and records parameters; it does not physically position the patient.
  • Core QA team: radiation therapist, radiation oncologist, dosimetrist, and physicist — not the medical oncologist.
